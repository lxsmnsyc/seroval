- **Error**: `SerovalAbortedError`
- **Step**: none
- **Fields**: none.

## What it means

`deserialize` was called on a [cross deserializer](../streaming.md#receiving-a-json-stream) after its `abort` method. This error is never wrapped, so its production message has no step.

## Common causes

- Chunks still arrive after the connection was closed and the deserializer was aborted.

## How to fix it

- Stop reading from the source when you call `abort`.
- Do not pass chunks to a deserializer after you call its `abort` method.
