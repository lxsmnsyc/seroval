---
title: SRV009
description: Two nodes have the same id.
type: reference
---

- **Error**: `SerovalConflictedNodeIdError`
- **Step**: `deserialize`
- **Fields**: `nodeId`, the id that is used twice.

## What it means

Two nodes in the input declare a value under the same id. Each id may be declared once.

## Common causes

- One chunk of a cross-reference stream was decoded twice.
- Two senders share one `refs` map, or two streams share one deserializer.
- The input was not produced by seroval, or was changed on the way.

## How to fix it

- Decode each chunk once.
- Give each stream its own `refs` map and its own deserializer.
