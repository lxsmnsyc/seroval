title: SRV004
description: A required instance is missing.
---

- **Error**: `SerovalMissingInstanceError`
- **Step**: `deserialize`
- **Fields**: `tag`, either `Promise` or `Stream`.

## What it means

The input settles a promise or sends a value to a stream, but no promise or stream with that id was decoded before.

## Common causes

- Chunks of a cross-reference stream reach a deserializer that did not decode the first chunk.
- Chunks arrive out of order, or one was lost.
- Two streams share one deserializer, or one stream is split across two.
- The input was not produced by seroval, or was changed on the way.

## How to fix it

- Send every chunk, in order, to one [cross deserializer](../streaming.md#receiving-a-json-stream).
- Give each stream its own deserializer, and its own `scopeId` when the output is code.
